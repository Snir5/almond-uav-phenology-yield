  Interpretation:
  • Mean EBI_Norm is stable across years (0.544–0.552), indicating the affine
    radiometric correction successfully anchors cross-year comparability.
  • EBI_Norm σ declines monotonically: 2021 (0.106) > 2022 (0.083) > 2023 (0.054)
    > 2024 (0.047). This gradient reflects genuine differences in canopy spatial
    heterogeneity — 2021 had the widest bloom-greenness spread across trees;
    2024 (minimal chilling) showed the most uniform canopy state.
  • EBI_Z σ mirrors this pattern (0.519→0.231), confirming that among-tree
    variance, not cross-year bias, drives the year-to-year σ differences.
  • 2022 had a restricted dynamic range in the raw imagery (underexposure / low
    contrast, σ_pixel≈29 vs 44–59 for other years); its EBI_Z σ (0.411) is
    likely an underestimate of true biological variability.

  4. NGRDI–EBI CROSS-INDEX RELATIONSHIP
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  2021: Pearson r(EBI_Norm, NGRDI_Norm) = -0.5624  (n=1276)
  2022: Pearson r(EBI_Norm, NGRDI_Norm) = -0.7634  (n=1236)

  Interpretation:
  • The consistently negative r between EBI and NGRDI across all years confirms
    the expected physiological relationship: trees with higher EBI (brighter,
    more bloom-like canopy) show lower NGRDI (lower green/red ratio). This
    cross-validates both indices and suggests they capture orthogonal aspects
    of the same spectral gradient (vegetative greenness ↔ bloom brightness).

  Interpretation:
  • The 2021→2022 transition shows the largest Cohen's d (see table), consistent
    with 2022's stronger chilling (35 portions) potentially advancing/concentrating
    bloom relative to 2021's warmer winter.
  • The 2023→2024 transition reflects the dramatic chilling deficit of 2024
    (10 vs 25 portions); whether this produced delayed bloom or reduced bloom
    intensity cannot be inferred from EBI alone without phenological ground truth.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  6. LIMITATIONS AND CAVEATS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • n=4 years precludes statistical testing of climate–EBI relationships at the
    year level. Correlations in Fig. 3 are exploratory; significance requires
    a longer time series.
  • 2022 imagery has low contrast (σ_pixel≈29 vs 44–59 for other years); its EBI
    dynamic range is compressed and cross-year comparison should be treated with
    caution for that year specifically.
  • Rad_Feb = 0 for all years (column absent in CSV export); radiation-based
    features were excluded.
  • Climate data is station-level (no spatial gradient within Plot 1).
  • EBI is a spectral proxy for bloom brightness, not a direct pollen or flower
    count; ground-truth phenological data needed for full biological interpretation.
